Reproducing the run

Reproduce the table from the Threadon/ directory. The runner uses the compiler sources in compiler/, not the installed package. Core dumps are disabled by the runner itself.

cd Threadon
python3 benchmark/run.py --runs 3

Useful variants:

python3 benchmark/run.py --list
python3 benchmark/run.py --cases mandelbrot
python3 benchmark/run.py --langs cpp threadon-jit
python3 benchmark/run.py --runs 10
python3 benchmark/run.py --inline-threshold 1000
python3 benchmark/run.py --installed

Reading the columns

  • compile is wall time to build the artefact once; - means an interpreted language has no build step.
  • median and best are wall times over fresh process launches, so JIT startup is included in the Threadon (JIT/lli) rows.
  • result is the checksum printed by the program. Every implementation agreed in every case.
  • vs C++ is median divided by the C++ median. The C++ row is the baseline.
  • Small arithmetic cases such as dotint and matmul are dominated by ~0.1s of JIT startup, which is why the JIT ratio looks much worse there than in collatz.
  • Differences around 1.00x are noise on this machine. Re-run with --runs 10 before drawing conclusions.
